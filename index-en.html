What is Lymphatic Drainage?

Lymphatic drainage is a gentle and non-invasive body care technique. Through soft and rhythmic touch, it supports the body’s natural circulation and relaxation. This service does not involve deep muscle manipulation and is not considered a medical treatment. It is especially suitable for people who require gentle and attentive body care.

Practical Benefits of Supportive Care

  • Helps the body relax and improve overall comfort
  • Supports the natural function of the circulatory system
  • Provides gentle touch for individuals with limited mobility
  • Encourages body awareness without adding physical stress

Lymphatic Drainage vs Traditional Massage

Category Traditional Massage Lymphatic Drainage
Main Purpose Relieve muscle soreness and tension Support circulation and immune system
Body System Muscles and fascia Lymphatic and circulatory system
Technique Deep pressure or trigger point techniques Gentle rhythmic movements
Body Sensation Sometimes intense or painful Light, relaxing and comfortable
Suitable For Sports recovery or muscle tension Most people, including postpartum and elderly
Body Impact Local muscle relief Whole-body circulation support
Overall Result Short-term muscle relaxation Long-term circulation balance

Lymphatic drainage is a supportive wellness practice and not a medical treatment.
